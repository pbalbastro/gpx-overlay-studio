    How to use

    How to use

    1. Get your GPX. Strava: open your activity on strava.com → ⋯ menu → Export GPX. Garmin: open the activity on Garmin Connect → ⚙ → Export to GPX. On a phone, use the website (not the app) and save the file to Files.
    2. Upload it. Tap GPX at the top, or drop the file into the upload box.
    3. Pick a template. Switch tabs and swipe or click through the designs.
    4. Save & share. Save the PNG (or Copy it), then add it over your photo in Instagram Stories. The background stays transparent.
